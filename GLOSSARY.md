# Pi Legal Workbench

Project-local legal matters, their drafting alternatives, and reviewable work product.

## Language

**Matter**:
A user-chosen legal engagement or internal task whose materials and scope are kept separate from unrelated work.
_Avoid_: session (a matter may span several sessions)

**Drafting anchor**:
The original or expressly selected source state used as a reference for substantive drafting, distinct from the immediately preceding version.

**Chapter original**:
The source text before a chapter's substantive drafting series, consulted when changing its drafting logic rather than continuing its latest argument.
_Avoid_: latest proposal (unless expressly adopted)

**Proposal parent**:
The textual state immediately preceding a revision, which may contain earlier drafted logic even when that logic is not reused as the new revision's drafting reference.
_Avoid_: original baseline (when referring to a refinement's immediate predecessor)

**Document checkpoint**:
A preserved textual state after a bounded drafting change. Its recording time is not evidence of an underlying legal event's date.
_Avoid_: verified legal event

**Delivery artifact**:
An expressly requested handoff file derived from an identified draft, distinct from the working text used during revision.
_Avoid_: new working draft (when referring to a requested Word delivery)

**Matter lifecycle record**:
An authoritative record of the matter's organizational state and history, independent of experimental wording alternatives.
_Avoid_: proposal text
