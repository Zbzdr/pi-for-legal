# Contract review memo format

Use this order unless the user requests a different deliverable.

```markdown
> **Reviewer note**
> - Profile: [path and active side | no playbook]
> - Sources/read coverage: [files and portions read; missing materials]
> - External legal sources: [successfully used sources | none]
> - Flagged for judgment: [count and short description]
> - Before relying: [specific verification or approval steps]

[Privilege/confidentiality marking approved in the profile; omit or qualify if none]

# Contract Review — [deal/document]

## Routing

| Document | Type | Workflow | Read coverage |
|---|---|---|---|

## Bottom line

[Decision-useful summary; do not make the decision for the lawyer.]

## Deal-breakers and required escalations

[The active side's "one thing" first.]

## Risk overview

| Topic | Legal risk | Business friction | Status | Owner |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|

## Findings

### [Finding]

- **Severity:** blocking / high / medium / low / favorable
- **Type:** legal risk / business friction / both
- **Contract:** "[exact quote]" — [section/page/file]
- **Playbook:** [exact profile position or no position]
- **Why it matters:** [deal-specific analysis]
- **Recommendation:** [accept / clarify / redline / escalate / obtain facts]
- **Proposed language:** [draft language, if appropriate]
- **Owner/approver:** [name or role]
- **Open fact:** [if any]

## Favorable terms

## Missing, conflicting, and incorporated terms

## Business terms and dates

| Item | Value | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Legal authorities

[Only where law materially drives a finding. Include source provenance and verification status.]

## Open questions

## Recommended sequence

1. [first recoverable step]
2. [next step]

**One question outside the checklist:** [a genuine second-order question, or omit]

## What next?

[Tailored options: draft language, escalate, get facts, track renewal, or another user-chosen action.]
```

Never call a citation verified merely because it appears in an agreement or can be retrieved. Never call a position "market" without a cited, current source or an explicit profile basis.
