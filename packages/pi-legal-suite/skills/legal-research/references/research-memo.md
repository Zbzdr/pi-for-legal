# Internal legal research memo format

```markdown
> **Reviewer note**
> - Scope: [question, jurisdiction, forum, as-of date]
> - Sources searched: [only sources actually searched]
> - Coverage: [date ranges, databases, local files, gaps]
> - Citator/currentness: [completed with source | limited | not completed]
> - Before relying: [specific verification or judgment steps]

[Privilege/confidentiality marking approved in the profile, if any]

# Research Memorandum — [question]

## Question presented

## Material facts and assumptions

- Known:
- Assumed:
- Missing:

## Short answer

[Answer with confidence and principal condition; no false certainty.]

## Authority map

| Authority | Court/issuer | Date | Weight/status | Treatment check | Proposition | Provenance/locator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Analysis

### [Issue]

**Rule.** [Supported rule.]

**Application.** [Apply only established facts; identify assumptions.]

**Counterarguments/adverse authority.** [Best opposing authority and distinctions.]

**Interim conclusion.** [Calibrated conclusion.]

## Uncertainty and coverage limits

## Questions for reviewing counsel

## Next steps
```

For a roadmap rather than a memo, use: framed question, primary-law starting points, case-law areas, administrative sources, secondary orientation sources, proposed search strings, uncertainty flags, and a verification checklist. A roadmap contains leads, not authorities, unless a cited item was actually retrieved and checked.
