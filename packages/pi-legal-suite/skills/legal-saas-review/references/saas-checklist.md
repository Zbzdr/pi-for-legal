# SaaS overlay issue map

## Economics and subscription

- committed spend, usage metrics, minimums, true-ups and overages;
- initial term, automatic renewal, cancel window and notice method;
- price increases, notice, caps, benchmarking and repricing;
- order-form/MSA precedence and online-term change rights;
- taxes, credits, refunds, disputed invoices and setoff.

## Service and remedies

- uptime definition, exclusions, measurement and reporting;
- severity levels, response/restoration targets and support hours;
- service credits: automatic or claimed, caps, sole-remedy effect;
- chronic failure termination and transition assistance;
- suspension triggers, notice, proportionality and emergency exceptions.

## Security and privacy

- data roles, instructions, purpose limits and DPA consistency;
- security standard, certifications, testing and remediation;
- incident definition, notice trigger/timing, content and cooperation;
- subprocessor list, notice, objection and replacement rights;
- data location, cross-border transfers and government requests;
- audit/assurance, regulator and customer cooperation;
- deletion, return, backup retention and legal holds.

## Data and AI

- customer data ownership and licenses;
- telemetry, product improvement, benchmarking and de-identification;
- model training, fine-tuning, human review, prompts and outputs;
- derivative or aggregated data rights and re-identification controls;
- output ownership, infringement allocation and acceptable use.

## Exit and continuity

- export format, timing, fees and assistance;
- deletion certificate and residual copies;
- business continuity, disaster recovery and force majeure;
- assignment/change of control, subcontracting and vendor lock-in;
- survival of data, confidentiality, payment and liability terms.
