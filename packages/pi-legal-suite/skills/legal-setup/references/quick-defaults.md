# Quick-start defaults

Use these only after showing them to the user. Prefix every accepted value with `[DEFAULT — generic starting point]`. A default is provisional workflow guidance, not company policy, market practice, delegated authority, or a verified statement of law.

## Shared

- Risk posture: middle, with uncertain subjective calls flagged `[review]`.
- Research: identify the controlling jurisdiction and as-of date; prefer primary and official sources; label unretrieved propositions `[model knowledge — verify]`; never claim a citator/currentness check that did not occur.
- Authority: no assumed signer, approver, settlement authority, filing authority, or dollar threshold. Ask when consequential.
- Output: concise internal memo, exact quotations and source anchors, favorable terms as well as risks, and explicit open facts.
- External action: draft only until the user confirms the destination and authorization.
- Matter isolation: off for a single-client in-house workspace; on for firm, solo, clinic, or multi-client work unless the user chooses otherwise.

## Commercial

- Confirm sales or purchasing side before applying positions; keep the two sides separate.
- Treat uncapped liability, broad indemnity, IP transfer, customer-data training, missing security/data-return terms, unilateral suspension, silent online-term changes, and renewal traps as review or escalation issues.
- Do not assume a liability-cap multiple, governing law, notice period, or approval threshold. Propose balanced fallback language and mark it as a draft for lawyer judgment.
- Confirm document routing by default and produce one integrated memo for the agreement set.

## Privacy

- Determine controller/processor or equivalent role, data subjects, data categories, locations, transfers, sector, and applicable regimes before classification.
- Use a conservative intake gate for rights requests, breach, sensitive data, children, biometrics, automated decisions, and cross-border transfers.
- Do not calculate a statutory deadline until jurisdiction, receipt date, identity-verification status, and extension rules are established.

## Regulatory

- Watch only named regulators, jurisdictions, products, and topics.
- Treat new binding duties, enforcement, effective dates, comment deadlines, licensing changes, and conflicts with current policy as material; treat speeches and informal commentary as lower priority unless the user says otherwise.
- Default digest cadence: weekly; urgent deadline or enforcement items surface immediately.

## AI governance

- Inventory the system, role, purpose, affected people, data, deployment locations, vendor chain, and applicable regimes before assigning a risk tier.
- Escalate prohibited or high-impact uses, employment/education/credit/health/safety uses, biometric or sensitive-data uses, opaque automated decisions, material model changes, and vendor training on protected data.
- Use a structured impact assessment and conditional approval rather than silently approving an uncertain use case.

## Employment

- Confirm work location and governing regime before applying hiring, termination, wage-hour, leave, classification, restrictive-covenant, or investigation rules.
- Do not issue a clear-to-hire or clear-to-terminate conclusion with missing protected-activity, leave/accommodation, contract, policy, documentation, consultation, or notice facts.
- Preserve investigation confidentiality and use attorney review for privilege-sensitive or consequential steps.

## Corporate

- Use a materiality-filtered issue list, source-linked diligence findings, explicit owners, and open conditions.
- Do not assume signing authority, board approval, filing deadline, disclosure threshold, or entity status.
- Draft consents, minutes, checklists, schedules, and integration plans for review; do not mark them final or filed.

## Litigation

- Use a 3×3 severity/likelihood matrix as a starting framework and flag uncertain privilege calls for attorney review.
- Confirm forum, posture, service/receipt date, operative rules, deadlines, preservation duties, insurance, and authority before action.
- Grand-jury, criminal, sanctions, imminent filing, preservation failure, and limitations issues receive immediate escalation.

## Intellectual property

- Default enforcement posture: measured; confirm rights, ownership, territory, status, evidence, business relationship, and approval before sending.
- Clearance, FTO, infringement, takedown, OSS, and portfolio outputs are screening or workflow artifacts unless qualified counsel adopts a final opinion.
- Never treat a search limited to one registry, class, jurisdiction, claim set, or date range as complete.

## Product

- Use the upstream seven-category launch framework when no house framework exists.
- Default risk posture: middle; block or escalate unresolved illegality, safety, deception, children, sensitive data, discrimination, licensing, sanctions, and missing claim substantiation.
- Draft launch conditions with owners and evidence needed; do not silently convert uncertainty into approval.
