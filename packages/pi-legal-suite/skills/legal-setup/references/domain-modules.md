# Practice-module profile sections

Use only the modules selected by the user. Ask one coherent question at a time, reuse supplied documents, and label every unresolved field `Not configured — ask at use time`.

## Privacy

- Regulatory footprint and sectoral overlays
- DPA roles and separate processor/controller positions
- Required terms, acceptable fallbacks, automatic escalation, and the one thing
- DSAR intake, identity verification, systems, exemptions, owners, and deadline controls
- PIA/DPIA triggers, house format, risk scale, approvers, and policy commitments
- Privacy notices, data maps, retention rules, transfer mechanisms, and source documents

## Regulatory

- Regulators, topics, jurisdictions, entities, products, and watchlist exclusions
- Materiality threshold: what creates an alert, escalation, owner, or policy review
- Policy library locations, owners, versions, and indexing status
- Preferred primary feeds, update cadence, comment-period process, and source hierarchy
- Gap statuses, remediation owners, risk acceptance authority, and notification rules

## AI governance

- AI system inventory fields, company role, uses, owners, data, jurisdictions, and risk taxonomy
- Approved, conditional, and prohibited use cases
- Impact-assessment triggers, method, format, reviewers, and decision authority
- Vendor AI positions: training, data use, security, audit, IP, indemnity, transparency, and exit
- AI policy commitments, exceptions, monitoring, incident routing, and regulatory footprint

## Employment

- Workforce jurisdictions, worker types, headcount, handbooks, and state/country supplements
- Hiring and restrictive-covenant triggers, templates, and approval rules
- Termination/RIF risk factors, severance framework, decision authority, and escalation
- Wage-hour, leave, accommodation, classification, and policy owners
- Investigation protocol, privilege model, evidence storage, audience controls, and reporters
- International expansion tracker, advisors, entities/PEO/EOR posture, and launch gates

## Corporate

- Active modules: M&A, board/secretary, public company, entity management, integration
- Deal-team roles, diligence taxonomy, materiality, issue format, and approval thresholds
- Board/consent precedents, signatory rules, minute style, and repository
- Entity register, jurisdictions, filing agents, deadlines, and system of record
- Closing and post-closing templates, required-consent process, owners, and reporting cadence

## Litigation

- Matter taxonomy, risk scale, materiality, stages, owners, and outside-counsel conventions
- Intake fields, matter log, status cadence, deadlines, reserves, and escalation triggers
- Privilege and work-product conventions, legal-hold process, custodians, and release authority
- Demand, subpoena, chronology, claim-chart, deposition, and briefing formats
- Citation standard, forum-specific research requirements, and external communication approvals

## Intellectual property

- Active rights: trademark, copyright, patent, trade secret, domain, and open source
- Jurisdictions, portfolio systems, prosecution counsel, deadlines, and source of truth
- Clearance/FTO thresholds, search tools, confidence labels, and opinion authority
- Enforcement posture, counterparty diligence, demand/takedown approvals, and escalation
- Invention intake, bar-date process, assignment rules, clause positions, and OSS policy

## Product

- Product lines, launch stages, intake channels, jurisdictions, owners, and review triggers
- Risk taxonomy and launch authority; what is block, conditional, escalate, or monitor
- Marketing-claim substantiation standard, prohibited claims, evidence owners, and approvals
- Privacy, AI, IP, consumer-protection, accessibility, safety, and regulated-feature handoffs
