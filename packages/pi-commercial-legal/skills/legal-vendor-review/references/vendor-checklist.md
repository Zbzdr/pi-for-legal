# Vendor and services agreement issue map

This is an issue map, not a source of default outcomes. Apply the project's active-side playbook.

## Document mechanics

- correct legal entities, affiliates, authority, effective date, signatures;
- complete document set and incorporated URLs;
- definitions, precedence, amendment mechanics, conflicting exhibits;
- scope, deliverables, acceptance, dependencies, change control;
- fees, taxes, expenses, invoices, disputes, setoff, late charges.

## Risk allocation

- warranties, disclaimers, exclusive remedies;
- limitation of liability: direct cap, consequential damages, carveouts, cap base;
- indemnity: covered claims, direction, exclusions, procedure, control, settlement;
- insurance and evidence of coverage;
- equitable relief, fee shifting, cumulative remedies.

## IP and information

- background IP, work product, deliverables, ownership, licenses;
- feedback, residuals, open-source components, moral rights;
- confidentiality definition, exclusions, compelled disclosure, duration;
- publicity, names, marks, benchmarking, aggregated or de-identified data.

## Data, security, and compliance

- controller/processor roles and DPA consistency;
- security standard, audits, certifications, incident notice and cooperation;
- subprocessors, locations, transfer restrictions, deletion/return;
- AI/model training, product improvement, telemetry, derivative data;
- applicable-law promises, anti-bribery, sanctions/export, accessibility;
- records, audit rights, regulatory cooperation.

## Lifecycle and disputes

- initial term, renewal, notice window, price changes;
- termination for cause/convenience, cure, suspension, transition;
- effect of termination, refunds, wind-down, data export;
- assignment, change of control, subcontracting;
- force majeure and business continuity;
- governing law, venue, arbitration, jury/class waivers, notice;
- survival and order-of-precedence after termination.

## Commercial reality

- obligations depend on facts the legal reviewer does not have;
- service level and remedy match operational importance;
- financial exposure matches deal value and insurance;
- commitments align with product, security, finance, and procurement practice;
- named internal owners exist for recurring duties and deadlines.
