# NDA issue map

- parties, affiliates, representatives, and permitted recipients;
- mutual versus one-way structure and the user's actual role;
- definition and marking requirements for confidential information;
- oral disclosures and confirmation windows;
- standard exclusions and burden of proof;
- permitted purpose and use restrictions;
- disclosure to representatives and responsibility for them;
- compelled disclosure notice and cooperation;
- residuals and unaided-memory rights;
- term of agreement, confidentiality survival, trade-secret treatment;
- return/destruction, backup, legal hold, archival and certification duties;
- ownership, no license, feedback, reverse engineering;
- non-solicit, non-compete, exclusivity, standstill or no-contact terms;
- representations, accuracy, reliance and clean-team issues;
- remedies, injunctions, bonds, damages and fee shifting;
- governing law, forum, assignment, notices and publicity;
- data protection or security obligations hidden in the NDA;
- conflicts with an existing NDA, MSA, clean-team agreement, or policy.
